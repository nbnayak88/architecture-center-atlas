# Navratri Special: Ashtalakshmi and Maa Saraswati

> **Session learning journal** · Teacher: Vani-ji · Theme: Prosperity, wisdom, devotion and conscious practice

## Central insight

Prosperity is multidimensional: health, nourishment, courage, dignity, family welfare, success, discernment and material abundance. Listening to the descriptions in the *Ashtalakshmi Stotram* and *Saraswati Stotram* helps us consciously remember the qualities we invoke.

> **Practice:** Remember at least one divine quality during puja and express it through a daily choice.

## The eight forms of Mahalakshmi

| Form | Dimension of prosperity | Everyday expression |
|---|---|---|
| Adi Lakshmi | Health, life and inner alignment | Gratitude, sweet speech and virtue |
| Dhanya Lakshmi | Food and nourishment | Respect food, nourish others, practise auspicious habits |
| Dhairya Lakshmi | Courage and faith | Act despite fear, supported by knowledge and surrender |
| Gaja Lakshmi | Dignity, power and recognition | Use influence and wealth according to dharma |
| Santana Lakshmi | Family welfare and legacy | Extend affection and goodwill to all |
| Vijaya Lakshmi | Victory and completion | Bring meaningful undertakings to completion |
| Vidya Lakshmi | Wisdom and discernment | Translate knowledge into sound choices |
| Dhana Lakshmi | Material wealth and abundance | Steward resources responsibly and generously |

### 1. Adi Lakshmi — Health, Life and Inner Alignment

- **Chandra Sahodari:** Sister of the moon; evokes the traditional ocean-of-milk imagery.
- **Moksha Pradayini:** Giver of liberation from the cycle of birth and death.
- **Manjula Bhashini:** Sweet-spoken; an invitation to practise gentle speech.
- **Sadguna Varshini:** Bestower of virtues.

**Reflection:** Breathing, living, health and inner connectedness are forms of wealth worthy of gratitude.

### 2. Dhanya Lakshmi — Food, Nourishment and Auspiciousness

- **Kali Kalmasha Nashini:** Destroyer of Kali's impurities. The session discussed the four vices of meat-eating, intoxication, illicit sexual conduct and gambling within its devotional framework.
- **Vaidika Rupini:** Embodiment of Vedic tradition and dharmic practice.
- **Mantra Nivasini:** One who resides in sacred mantras.
- **Mangala Dayini:** Giver of auspiciousness.

**Reflection:** Honour the food that sustains life; make chanting and nourishment conscious practices.

### 3. Dhairya Lakshmi — Courage and Faith

- **Jnana Vikasini:** Expander of knowledge; competence supports confidence.
- **Bhava Bhaya Harini:** Remover of existential fear, including fear of loss and uncertainty.
- **Papa Vimochini:** Remover of sins, in the devotional understanding of surrender.
- **Mantra Svarupini:** The embodiment of mantra itself.

> “When we act in spite of fear, it is called courage.”

**Reflection:** Courage is not the absence of fear. Begin meaningful work with preparation, faith and surrender.

### 4. Gaja Lakshmi — Dignity, Power and Dharma

- **Ratha, Gaja, Turaga, Padati:** Chariots, elephants, horses and infantry, symbolising sovereign resources.
- **Durgati Nashini:** Remover of misfortune and inauspicious destiny.
- **Shastramaye:** Associated with scriptural wisdom guiding power.
- **Tapa Nivarini:** Remover of suffering.

**Reflection:** Wealth and devotion can coexist. Status, beauty, intelligence and resources require dharmic stewardship; material abundance alone does not guarantee fulfilment.

### 5. Santana Lakshmi — Affection, Virtues and Family Welfare

- **Guna Gana Varidhi:** Ocean of virtues such as kindness, hospitality and generosity.
- **Lokahitaiṣini:** Well-wisher of the world; expand prayers beyond one's household.
- **Saptaswara Bhushita Gana:** Praised through the seven musical notes.
- **Raga Vivardhini:** One who increases affection and devotional absorption.

> “Loka Samasta Sukhino Bhavantu.”

**Reflection:** When irritated with others, pray for the capacity to respond with greater affection. Include neighbours, communities and all living beings in goodwill.

### 6. Vijaya Lakshmi — Victory, Success and Completion

- **Sadgati Dayini:** Giver of auspicious destination and spiritual progress.
- **Kumkuma Dhusara Bhushita:** Adorned with kumkum.
- **Gaanamayi:** Associated with music and praise.
- **Kanakadhara Stuti Vaibhava Vandita:** Linked in the session to the traditional *Kanakadhara Stotram* narrative.

**The Kanakadhara story:** A poor woman offered Adi Shankaracharya her only amla as alms. Moved by her generosity, he recited the *Kanakadhara Stotram*; the traditional account describes a shower of golden amlas. The lesson is generous action even amid scarcity. The session associated this blessing with Vijaya Lakshmi.

**Reflection:** *Karya-siddhi* requires not only starting but completing worthwhile undertakings.

### 7. Vidya Lakshmi — Wisdom and Discernment

- **Bharati:** A name associated with Saraswati, speech and knowledge.
- **Shoka Vinashini:** Remover of sorrow.
- **Shanti Samavruta Hasyamukhe:** A peaceful, smiling countenance.
- **Navanidhi Dayini:** Giver of nine treasures.

**Reflection:** Information alone does not make us wise. Discernment is visible when understanding changes behaviour.

### 8. Dhana Lakshmi — Material Abundance

- **Dundubhi Nada and Shankha Ninada:** Sounds of drums and conch, invoking sacred celebration.
- **Veda Purana Itihasa Supujita:** Revered in Vedas, Puranas and Itihasas.
- **Vaidika Marga Pradarshayati:** One who shows the Vedic path.

**Reflection:** Money, gold, land and property are resources to receive with gratitude and use responsibly.

## Vaibhav Lakshmi Puja — Practising with awareness

The teacher recognised both first-time participants and those continuing their weekly practice. Beginning an unfamiliar puja itself exemplifies **Dhairya Lakshmi**. Repetition develops familiarity and devotional connection.

**Simple method:** Before the invocation, choose one Lakshmi, recall one quality, and resolve to express it in a concrete action.

## Maa Saraswati — Purity, Harmony and Spiritual Understanding

### White radiance: purity and clarity

**Ya Kundendu Tushara Hara Dhavala** evokes the whiteness of jasmine, moonlight, frost and pearls. White garments and lotus represent purity, peace and a mind less clouded by prejudice and agitation.

### The veena: creativity and refined expression

**Ya Veena Vara Danda Mandita Kara** portrays Saraswati holding a veena, symbolising music, artistic expression and harmony. The session shared the traditional association of keeping a musical instrument at home with Saraswati's presence.

### The white lotus: spiritual grounding

**Ya Shweta Padmasana** describes Saraswati seated on a white lotus. Visualise serenity and spiritual clarity while continuing worldly responsibilities.

### Removing ignorance and dullness

**Nihshesha Jadyapaha** invokes the removal of ignorance and dullness. Beyond concentration and academic learning, the teacher emphasised awareness of life's impermanence and our relationship with the Divine. Fulfil family duties with care without mistaking temporary possessions for ultimate identity.

## Devotional service as a source of fulfilment

Small actions can become profound offerings when performed with devotion:

- Light a lamp.
- Replace flowers at the home altar.
- Clean the altar or temple floor.
- Serve prasad.
- Chant and remember the Lord.

The teacher described learning, teaching and service as sources of purpose and happiness.

## Seven-day reflection experiment

| Day | Focus | Experiment |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Adi + Dhanya | Express gratitude for health and food; speak gently |
| 2 | Dhairya | Take one meaningful step despite hesitation |
| 3 | Gaja | Review whether one use of authority aligns with dharma |
| 4 | Santana | Offer goodwill to someone outside the immediate family |
| 5 | Vijaya | Complete one pending meaningful task |
| 6 | Vidya | Change one action based on something already known |
| 7 | Dhana + Saraswati | Practise generosity, study and a small devotional service |

**Journal prompts:** Which quality did I invoke? What choice expressed it? What changed in my awareness? What will I carry forward?

## Closing takeaway

**Understand the qualities being invoked, remember them during worship, and embody them in everyday decisions:** sweet speech, nourishment, courage, dharmic power, affection, completion, discernment and generosity.
