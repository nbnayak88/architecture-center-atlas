# 2. Business Architect (BA)

## WHAT

Shapes business models, value streams, processes, capabilities, and organizational design.

## WHY

To ensure operations, strategy, and technology investments reflect real business priorities and customer value.

## HOW

- Model business capabilities and value streams
- Conduct process redesign and optimization
- Support strategic planning and change adoption
- Translate business goals into architectural requirements

## Process Architect specialization

Process Architect belongs within the Business Architect path. It focuses on process architecture, value streams, role handoffs, controls, process improvement and measurable business outcomes. Process Bootcamps can apply SAP Signavio and a specific application such as SAP SuccessFactors or SAP Ariba; the architecture foundation remains vendor-neutral.
