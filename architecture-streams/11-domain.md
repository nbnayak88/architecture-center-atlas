# 11. Domain Architect (DoA)

## WHAT

Focuses deeply on a specific business domain (HR, Finance, CRM, Supply Chain, Manufacturing, Billing, etc.).

## WHY

Domain mastery ensures architecture reflects real business operations, regulatory needs, and industry best practices.

## HOW

- Build domain capability models & process maps
- Govern domain-specific applications
- Map regulatory & compliance requirements
- Provide cross-functional subject matter expertise

This architecture stream is a vendor- and product-agnostic foundation for **11 GICS sectors × 20 Labs = 220 journeys**. Apply relevant global standards (TOGAF, BIZBOK, BABOK, DAMA-DMBOK, APQC, GICS, and other discipline-specific references) based on the learning context.
