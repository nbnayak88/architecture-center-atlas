# 8. UI/UX Architect (UXA)

## WHAT

Architects human-centered interfaces, digital experiences, accessibility, and design systems.

## WHY

Because usability, accessibility, and experience quality drive adoption, satisfaction, and business success.

## HOW

- Build design systems & component libraries
- Conduct user research, journey mapping, usability tests
- Define interaction models & accessibility standards
- Align UI/UX patterns across products and platforms
