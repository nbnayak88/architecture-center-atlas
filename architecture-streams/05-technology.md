# 5. Technology Architect (TA)

## WHAT

Defines the technology platforms, tools, frameworks, and stack used across the enterprise.

## WHY

To ensure the tech stack is future-proof, secure, cost-effective, and aligned with emerging technologies.

## HOW

- Define the enterprise technology stack & standards
- Evaluate emerging technologies & conduct POCs
- Govern platform selection & lifecycle management
- Optimize performance, scalability, and cost
