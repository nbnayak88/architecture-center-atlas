# 6. AI Architect (AIA)

## WHAT

Designs AI/ML ecosystems, model pipelines, governance frameworks, and ethical AI implementation.

## WHY

AI is a competitive differentiator, but needs strong governance, quality, and alignment to avoid bias, risk, and failure.

## HOW

- Build ML pipelines and MLOps frameworks
- Implement model governance, monitoring, and ethics
- Align AI use cases with business value
- Integrate models with applications and data platforms
