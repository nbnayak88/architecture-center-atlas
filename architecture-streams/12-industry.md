# 12. Industry Architect (IAc)

## WHAT

Owns architecture for an end-to-end industry vertical (Energy, Healthcare, Banking, Retail, Utilities, etc.).

## WHY

Industry-specific complexities require specialized patterns, regulations, and best practices that generic architects cannot address.

## HOW

- Build industry reference architectures
- Map ecosystem, regulatory bodies, and industry workflows
- Design industry-specific solutions & innovations
- Enable cross-industry benchmarking and competitiveness
