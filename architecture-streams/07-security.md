# 7. Security Architect (SA)

## WHAT

Creates the security frameworks, identity systems, compliance structures, and zero-trust architecture.

## WHY

To protect assets, data, and operations from threats, ensuring trust, compliance, and resilience.

## HOW

- Design identity access & zero-trust frameworks
- Implement cloud, app, and data security patterns
- Map regulatory compliance (GDPR, SOC, ISO, etc.)
- Conduct risk assessments & threat modeling
