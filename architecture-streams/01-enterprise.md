# 1. Enterprise Architect (EA)

## WHAT

Designs the enterprise-wide blueprint connecting strategy, business, data, applications, technologies, and governance into a unified architecture.

## WHY

To ensure the entire organization operates as a coherent system, reducing redundancy, aligning decisions to strategy, and enabling scalable transformation.

## HOW

- Define enterprise capability models & value chains
- Establish architectural governance & standards
- Create multi-year transformation roadmaps
- Align business objectives with technology investments
