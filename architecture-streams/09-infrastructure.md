# 9. Infrastructure Architect (InfraA)

## WHAT

Designs cloud, hybrid, and on-prem infrastructure including compute, network, storage, and operations.

## WHY

## HOW

- Architect cloud/hybrid infrastructure & topology
- Implement DevOps, IaC, monitoring & observability
- Govern reliability, performance, and availability
- Ensure cost optimization & disaster recovery
