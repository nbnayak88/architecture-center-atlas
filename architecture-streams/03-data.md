# 3. Data Architect (DA)

## WHAT

Designs the data models, data pipelines, governance frameworks, and analytical ecosystems.

## WHY

Because data is a strategic asset—organizations need trusted, consistent, scalable data to drive insights and innovation.

## HOW

- Build conceptual, logical, and physical data models
- Design warehouses, lakes, lakehouses, and pipelines
- Ensure data quality, lineage, and governance
- Enable analytics, BI, and AI readiness
